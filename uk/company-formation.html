UK Company Formation

Limited company set up at Companies House, including for overseas founders.

Get a quote for thisCall +91 70489 15155WhatsApp

We help you form a private limited company, prepare the articles and statutory registers and register for corporation tax, PAYE and VAT where required.

What we handle

  • Company name check and incorporation
  • Share structure and statutory registers
  • Corporation tax, PAYE and VAT registration support
  • Confirmation statement reminders

What we need from you

  • Proposed name and business activity
  • Director and shareholder details with ID
  • A UK registered office address

Who files. We prepare the books, VAT workings, accounts and returns. Submission to HMRC and Companies House is made by you, or through your UK accountant or our UK associates, as you prefer.

How the process works

  1. Tell us what you need. Send the form or message us on WhatsApp. We reply within one working day.
  2. Agree scope and a fixed fee. You get a written scope, timeline and fee in your currency before work begins.
  3. Share access and documents. We work inside your accounting software or from documents you upload.
  4. Review and file. You review the finished work. Filing is done by you or your local adviser, or by us in India.

Frequently asked questions

Can someone living in India own a UK company?

Yes. A UK company can have overseas directors and shareholders, though it needs a UK registered office address and identity verification.

How do we work together across time zones?

Our office works 7 AM to 7 PM India time, Monday to Saturday. We agree a reply window that overlaps with your day, and handle everything by email, WhatsApp and video call.

How are fees quoted and paid?

We quote a fixed fee in pounds sterling before starting. Payment is by international bank transfer or card.
