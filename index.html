Questions people ask before they call

Where is the Legal Khata office in Rohini?

We are at D-13/171, Second Floor, Sector 7, Rohini, Delhi 110085, a short ride from Rohini West and Rohini East metro stations. The office is open Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Your first consultation is free. We understand your requirement, tell you which documents are needed and give you a fixed quote before any work starts.

Do I have to visit the office?

No. Most work, including GST registration, income tax returns and company registration, is done online. You can send documents on WhatsApp or email, and we arrange pickup or a visit in and around Rohini when needed.

Which areas of Delhi do you serve?

Our office is in Rohini and we serve Pitampura, Netaji Subhash Place, Shalimar Bagh, Model Town and the rest of Delhi NCR. Clients in other states and countries work with us online.

How much do you charge?

Fees depend on the service and the volume of work. We quote a fixed fee in writing before starting, with government fees shown separately.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We file Indian returns for NRIs and provide accounting, tax and company formation support for clients in several countries. Use the country button at the top of the page to see the services for your country.
