We prepare your company registration and the tax registrations that follow.

What we handle

  • Name reservation and incorporation
  • IRD number and GST registration support
  • Shareholder and director consents
  • Annual return reminders

What we need from you

  • Director and shareholder details
  • Proposed name
  • New Zealand registered office address

Who files. We prepare the books, GST workings and returns. Filing with Inland Revenue is made by you or through your New Zealand accountant or tax agent.

How the process works

  1. Tell us what you need. Send the form or message us on WhatsApp. We reply within one working day.
  2. Agree scope and a fixed fee. You get a written scope, timeline and fee in your currency before work begins.
  3. Share access and documents. We work inside your accounting software or from documents you upload.
  4. Review and file. You review the finished work. Filing is done by you or your local adviser, or by us in India.

Frequently asked questions

Does a New Zealand company need a local director?

Yes. At least one director must live in New Zealand, or live in Australia and be a director of an Australian company.

How do we work together across time zones?

Our office works 7 AM to 7 PM India time, Monday to Saturday. We agree a reply window that overlaps with your day, and handle everything by email, WhatsApp and video call.

How are fees quoted and paid?

We quote a fixed fee in New Zealand dollars before starting. Payment is by international bank transfer or card.
