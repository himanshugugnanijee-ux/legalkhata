Company Setup Support in Saudi Arabia

Investment licence and commercial registration paperwork.

Get a quote for thisCall +91 70489 15155WhatsApp

We coordinate the documents for foreign investment licensing and commercial registration with advisers in the Kingdom.

What we handle

  • Structure options for foreign investors
  • Investment licence documentation
  • Commercial registration support
  • Parent company document attestation

What we need from you

  • Parent company documents
  • Shareholder passports
  • Planned activity

Who files. We prepare the books, VAT and zakat workings. Filing on the ZATCA portal is made with your authorisation or through your licensed adviser in the Kingdom.

How the process works

  1. Tell us what you need. Send the form or message us on WhatsApp. We reply within one working day.
  2. Agree scope and a fixed fee. You get a written scope, timeline and fee in your currency before work begins.
  3. Share access and documents. We work inside your accounting software or from documents you upload.
  4. Review and file. You review the finished work. Filing is done by you or your local adviser, or by us in India.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreign company own 100% of a Saudi entity?

In many sectors, yes, under a foreign investment licence. Some activities remain restricted.

How do we work together across time zones?

Our office works 7 AM to 7 PM India time, Monday to Saturday. We agree a reply window that overlaps with your day, and handle everything by email, WhatsApp and video call.

How are fees quoted and paid?

We quote a fixed fee in Saudi riyals before starting. Payment is by international bank transfer or card.
