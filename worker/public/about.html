About Legal Khata, tax and compliance consultants in Rohini

Who we are

Legal Khata is a business, tax and compliance consultancy working from Sector 7, Rohini, Delhi. Our team handles business registration, GST, income tax, TDS, company law compliance and bookkeeping for shop owners, professionals, start-ups, companies and salaried individuals.

How we work

  • You get a fixed quote in writing before any work starts.
  • One person is responsible for your file and answers your calls.
  • Documents can be sent on WhatsApp or email, or handed over at the office.
  • We remind you before each due date instead of after it.
  • You receive a copy of every acknowledgement, challan and certificate.

Who we work with

Most of our clients are in Rohini, Pitampura, Netaji Subhash Place, Shalimar Bagh and the rest of Delhi NCR. We also file Indian returns for NRIs and provide accounting and tax support to clients in other countries. Use the country button at the top of the page to see those services.
