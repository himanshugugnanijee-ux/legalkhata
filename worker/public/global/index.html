Common questions

Do I have to file a tax return in India if I live abroad?

You must file if your income arising in India is above the basic exemption limit, or if you want a refund of TDS deducted on rent, interest or property sale. Many NRIs file every year to keep their records clean.

Why was so much TDS deducted when I sold my flat in India?

Buyers must deduct tax at a high rate on the sale value when the seller is a non-resident. If your actual gain is lower, we apply for a lower deduction certificate before the sale, or claim the refund through your return.
