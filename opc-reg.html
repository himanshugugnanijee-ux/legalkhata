One Person Company Registration in Rohini, Delhi

A company with a single owner and a nominee, for solo founders.

Get a quote for thisCall +91 70489 15155WhatsApp

A one person company lets a single founder run a company with limited liability, without needing a second shareholder. You appoint one nominee who takes over only if you are unable to continue. It suits consultants, traders and solo founders who want a corporate identity.

What we handle

  • Name reservation and SPICe+ filing
  • Digital signature and DIN for the director
  • Nominee consent in Form INC-3
  • MoA and AoA for a one person company
  • PAN, TAN and post-incorporation compliance

Documents you need

  • PAN and Aadhaar of the owner and the nominee
  • Photographs and address proof of both
  • Registered office proof with NOC

Requirements vary with the type of business. We send you an exact checklist after a short call.

How the process works

  1. We confirm the name and nominee.
  2. The digital signature is issued.
  3. We file the incorporation documents.
  4. You receive the certificate of incorporation, PAN and TAN.

How long it takes

Commonly 7 to 15 working days.

Frequently asked questions

Who can form a one person company?

A natural person who is an Indian citizen, whether resident in India or not, can form an OPC, and can be a member of only one OPC at a time.

Can an OPC be converted into a private limited company?

Yes. An OPC can be converted by adding members and directors and filing the conversion forms.

Is an audit compulsory for an OPC?

Yes. Like every company, an OPC must have its accounts audited each year.
