Accounting and Bookkeeping in Rohini, Delhi

Monthly books in Tally or cloud software, with reports you can actually use.

Get a quote for thisCall +91 70489 15155WhatsApp

Clean books make GST returns, income tax, bank loans and audits straightforward. Our team maintains your accounts monthly, reconciles bank and GST data and gives you a simple profit and position report, so the year end is never a rush.

What we handle

  • Day-to-day entries in Tally, Zoho Books or QuickBooks
  • Bank, debtor and creditor reconciliation
  • Payroll, PF and ESI workings
  • Monthly profit and loss summary and outstanding reports
  • Year-end finalisation and audit support

Documents you need

  • Sales and purchase invoices
  • Bank statements
  • Expense bills and cash vouchers
  • Previous year's balance sheet and trial balance

Requirements vary with the type of business. We send you an exact checklist after a short call.

How the process works

  1. We study your business and set up the books.
  2. You share bills and statements weekly or monthly, by WhatsApp, email or pickup.
  3. We record, reconcile and raise queries.
  4. You receive monthly reports and a year-end balance sheet.

How long it takes

Monthly books are normally closed within the first two weeks of the following month.

Frequently asked questions

Do you send someone to our office?

Yes. For businesses in Rohini, Pitampura and nearby areas we can arrange a visiting accountant, or collect papers and work from our office.

Which accounting software do you use?

We work on Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks and Busy, and can continue on the software you already use.

Can you clean up books that are a year behind?

Yes. Backlog accounting is a regular part of our work, usually done alongside the pending returns.
